Get the idea right
before chasing finish.

The boards establish composition, action and the visual logic of the floating W-world before the workflow shifts into 3D previs, generation and final refinement.

Consistency is the craft.

Once the concept, art style and storyboard were established, the production challenge became realism and continuity. The workflow used 3D modelling and previsualisation to lock geometry, supported by style sheets for key scene elements.

That structure matters. It shifts the process away from repeatedly generating until something happens to look right, and toward a directed system with repeatable rules.
