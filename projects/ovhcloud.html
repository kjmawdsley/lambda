01 / OVHcloud / Campaign imagery

Outsmarting the hyperscalers.

A challenger cloud brand needed campaign imagery with the scale and polish of a major production — without inheriting the production footprint of one.

GenAICampaign imageryCreative development

Big-production thinking.
Leaner production.

The work used AI-assisted image generation as a production advantage rather than an aesthetic. The goal was simple: make the campaign feel expensive, intentional and fully art-directed — not generative for the sake of it.

The result is a useful example of where this approach earns its keep: ambitious visual concepts, fast exploration and high production value without defaulting to a conventional shoot.
